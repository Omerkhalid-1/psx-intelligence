PSX Intelligence

Educational research tool. Indicators describe what prices and volume did; they are not predictions, recommendations or investment advice. In this project's walk-forward tests, models built on these indicators did not forecast returns better than assuming no change.

Data

Daily bars and quotes: self-hosted psxdata-api reading the PSX Data Portal (subject to PSX terms). Prices are split/bonus-adjusted; cash dividends are not. Headlines: Google News RSS (titles and links only).
