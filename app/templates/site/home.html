A stock in five ideas

  1. Price is simply the last place a buyer and a seller agreed.
  2. Trend: moving averages smooth the noise so the direction shows.
  3. Momentum (RSI, MACD) measures how forcefully prices have been moving.
  4. Volatility (ATR, Bollinger Bands) measures how wide the swings are.
  5. Context: volume, the market index and past turning points.
